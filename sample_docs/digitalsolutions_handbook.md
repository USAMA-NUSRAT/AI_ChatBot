## 4. Services We Provide

DigitalSolutions provides end-to-end software development services.

### 4.1 Web Development

We build websites, web applications, dashboards, and e-commerce stores. Common technologies:

- **Frontend:** React, Next.js, Vue.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Backend:** Node.js, Python (FastAPI, Django), PHP (Laravel)
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, Redis
- **E-commerce:** Shopify, WooCommerce, custom stores

### 4.2 Mobile App Development

We build Android and iOS apps.

- **Cross-platform:** Flutter and React Native (our preferred approach for most clients)
- **Native:** Kotlin for Android, Swift for iOS
- We also handle publishing apps on the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

### 4.3 UI/UX Design

Our design team creates wireframes, prototypes, and complete design systems using Figma. Every project starts with a design phase unless the client provides final designs.

### 4.4 Artificial Intelligence and Chatbots

Since 2023, we build AI-powered solutions including customer support chatbots, document question-answering systems (RAG), and AI integrations using large language models such as Claude and Gemini.

### 4.5 Cloud and DevOps

We set up and manage cloud infrastructure on AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud, including CI/CD pipelines, Docker, and Kubernetes.

### 4.6 Quality Assurance (QA)

We offer manual and automated testing using tools such as Selenium, Cypress, and Playwright.

### 4.7 Maintenance and Support

After a project goes live, we offer monthly maintenance plans that include bug fixes, security updates, and small changes.

### Services We Do NOT Provide

- Hardware repair or sales
- Game development
- Blockchain and cryptocurrency projects
- Digital marketing and social media management

## 5. Pricing and Project Process

### 5.1 Pricing Models

1. **Fixed price** — for projects with a clear scope. 40% is paid upfront, 30% at the midpoint, and 30% on delivery.
2. **Hourly (Time and Materials)** — standard rate of USD 25 per hour for developers and USD 35 per hour for senior architects.
3. **Dedicated team** — a client hires a full team monthly. Starts at USD 3,500 per developer per month.

A simple business website usually starts from USD 1,500. A mobile app usually starts from USD 8,000. Final prices depend on features and complexity.

### 5.2 Project Process

Every project follows these steps:

1. **Discovery call** — a free 30-minute call to understand the client's needs.
2. **Proposal** — sent within 3 working days of the discovery call.
3. **Design** — wireframes and UI designs in Figma.
4. **Development** — work is done in 2-week sprints with a demo at the end of each sprint.
5. **Testing** — the QA team tests everything before release.
6. **Launch** — the project goes live.
7. **Support** — 30 days of free bug-fix support after launch.

### 5.3 Support Response Times (SLA)

| Priority | Example | First response time |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Website or app is down | Within 2 hours |
| High | A main feature is broken | Within 8 hours |
| Medium | A minor feature issue | Within 1 working day |
| Low | Small text or design change | Within 3 working days |

## 6. Salary and Payroll

- **Salary day:** Salaries are paid on the **1st of every month** for the previous month's work.
- If the 1st falls on a weekend or public holiday, salaries are paid on the **last working day before** it.
- Salaries are transferred directly to the employee's bank account. Cash payments are not made.
- **Payslips** are emailed to employees on the same day salaries are paid.
- **Income tax** is deducted at source according to the Government of Pakistan's tax rules.

### Annual Increments

Salary increments are given once a year in **July**, based on the annual performance review. Average increments range from 8% to 20%.

### Bonuses

- **Eid bonus:** Every permanent employee receives a bonus equal to 50% of their basic salary before Eid ul Fitr.
- **Project bonus:** Teams that deliver a project on time and within budget may receive a project completion bonus.
- **Referral bonus:** PKR 50,000 for referring a candidate who is hired and completes probation.

### Overtime

Overtime is paid at **1.5 times** the normal hourly rate. Overtime must be approved in advance by the team lead. Work on public holidays is paid at **2 times** the normal hourly rate.

### Advance Salary

Employees may request an advance of up to 50% of their monthly salary, once every 6 months, in case of emergency. It is deducted from the next salary.

## 11. Hiring and Onboarding

### 11.1 Hiring Process

1. Application through our careers page or email to hr@digitalsolutions.example
2. HR screening call (15 minutes)
3. Technical test (take-home, 48 hours to complete)
4. Technical interview with the team lead
5. Final interview with HR and the department head
6. Offer letter, usually within 5 working days of the final interview

### 11.2 Probation

- New employees are on probation for **3 months**.
- During probation, the notice period is **2 weeks**.
- After probation, the notice period is **1 month** for all employees, and **2 months** for team leads and managers.

### 11.3 First Day

On the first day, new employees:

- Receive their laptop, email account, and Slack access
- Meet their team lead and are assigned an "onboarding buddy"
- Attend a 1-hour orientation session with HR
- Sign the employment contract and non-disclosure agreement (NDA)

### 11.4 Internship Program

DigitalSolutions runs a **3-month paid internship program twice a year**, starting in **January and July**. Interns receive a monthly stipend of PKR 25,000. About 60% of interns are offered full-time jobs.

## 13. Performance Reviews

- Formal performance reviews are held **twice a year**, in **January** and **June**.
- The June review decides the July salary increment.
- Employees are rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Needs Improvement), 2 (Below Expectations), 3 (Meets Expectations), 4 (Exceeds Expectations), 5 (Outstanding).
- Employees rated 4 or 5 in two reviews in a row are considered for promotion.
- The **"Employee of the Quarter"** award includes a certificate and a cash prize of PKR 25,000.

## 15. IT and Security Policy

- Company laptops must be locked when unattended (Windows + L).
- All employees must use **two-factor authentication (2FA)** for email, Slack, and GitHub.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and changed every 90 days.
- Client code and data must never be uploaded to personal accounts, personal cloud storage, or public AI tools without client approval.
- USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops by default.
- Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT team within **2 hours**.
- The IT helpdesk can be reached on the Slack channel **#it-help**.
